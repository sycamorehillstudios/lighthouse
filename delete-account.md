# Delete your Lighthouse game data

Lighthouse: Idle Tower Defense is published by Sycamore Hill Studios. The game has no account and no servers of ours: your game is saved on your device and, if you sign in with Google Play Games, in your own Play Games cloud save.

## Delete from the game

1. Open Lighthouse.
2. Go to Settings → Delete my data.
3. Confirm. Your Play Games cloud save is deleted first, then the save, backups and settings on this device. If the cloud save cannot be reached, nothing is deleted and you can try again.

## Delete without the game

- **Data on your device:** uninstall the game. Everything it stored on the device is removed.
- **Play Games data (cloud save, achievements, scores):** in the Play Games app, open Settings → Delete Play Games account and data, and delete the data for Lighthouse (or all Play Games data). This is managed by Google with your Google account.

If you need help, email **sycamorehillstudios@outlook.com** with the subject "Delete my Lighthouse data".

## What is deleted

Your save (progress, currency balances, mission and pass progress), its backups, your settings and your Play Games cloud save for the game.

## What is not deleted by the game

- **Purchases** are recorded by Google Play under your Google account. Items you own (for example Skip Ads or the membership) come back with Restore purchases if you play again; pearls already delivered do not. Cancel an active subscription separately in Google Play.
- **Ad data** collected by Google AdMob is governed by Google's policies; you can reset your advertising ID in your device's settings.

We hold no copy of your game data, so there is nothing further for us to delete.
