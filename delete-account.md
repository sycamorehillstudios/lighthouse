# Delete your Lighthouse account and data

Lighthouse: Idle Tower Defense is published by Sycamore Hill Studios.

## Delete from the game

1. Open Lighthouse.
2. Go to Settings → Delete account.
3. Confirm. Your account and all its game data are deleted.

## Delete without the game

Email **sycamorehillstudios@outlook.com** with the subject "Delete my Lighthouse account". Include your player ID, shown in Settings → Account (or your Google Play Games display name if you signed in with it).

## What is deleted

Your account ID, save data, currency balances, mission and pass progress, tournament entries, station membership and ad-reward records.

## What may be kept

Purchase records required for tax and accounting law are kept for up to 7 years, without your game data. Deletion is completed within 30 days of your request.
