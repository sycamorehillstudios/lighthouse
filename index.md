# Privacy Policy for Lighthouse: Idle Tower Defense

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy describes how Sycamore Hill Studios ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game Lighthouse: Idle Tower Defense (the "game"), package `com.sycamorehillstudios.lighthouse`.

## Information we collect

- **Game account identifiers.** An anonymous account ID created by Firebase Authentication and, if you choose to sign in, your Google Play Games player ID and display name.
- **Game progress and purchases.** Your save data, in-game currency balances, purchase records and subscription status, so we can restore your progress and deliver what you buy.
- **Usage and diagnostics.** Game events (for example, a storm started or an upgrade bought), crash reports and performance data, collected through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics.
- **Advertising data.** If you choose to watch a rewarded ad, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction data. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise location or messages.

## How we use information

- To run the game, save your progress and restore it on a new device.
- To deliver purchases, subscriptions and rewards.
- To prevent fraud, cheating and abuse.
- To understand how the game is played and fix problems.
- To show a rewarded ad, only when you choose to watch one.

## Sharing

We use service providers that process data on our behalf under their own terms: Google Firebase, Google AdMob, Google Play Games Services and Google Play Billing. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for purposes other than those above.

## Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated game data at any time:

- In the game: Settings → Delete account.
- Without the game installed: follow the steps on our [data deletion page](delete-account).

Deletion is completed within 30 days. Records we must keep by law, such as purchase receipts, may be retained for longer.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to stored game data is restricted by server-side security rules.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to or restrict its processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
