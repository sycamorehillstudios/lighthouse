# Privacy Policy for Lighthouse: Idle Tower Defense

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy describes how Sycamore Hill Studios ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game Lighthouse: Idle Tower Defense (the "game"), package `com.sycamorehillstudios.lighthouse`.

**In short:** the game has no account and no servers of ours. Your game is saved on your device and, if you sign in with Google Play Games, in your own Play Games cloud save. We never receive your game data. The only data collected is collected by Google services the game uses: AdMob (only when you choose to watch a rewarded ad), Google Play Games Services and Google Play Billing.

## Information on your device

- **Your save.** Your progress, in-game currency balances, settings and a record of the purchases already delivered to this save (stored as one-way hashes of Google Play's purchase tokens) are kept in the game's private storage on your device, with a few automatic backups. They never leave your device unless you sign in with Play Games.
- **An install ID.** A random ID created by the game on your device, used only to check the device clock (so changing the clock cannot be used to cheat). It is not a device identifier and is never sent anywhere.
- **Your Google Play billing country.** Read from Google Play on your device to decide which items may be sold where you live. It is not sent anywhere.

## Information handled by Google services

- **Google Play Games Services (optional).** If you sign in with Play Games, Google provides your Play Games display name to the game, records your achievements and leaderboard scores, and keeps a copy of your save in Play Games Saved Games, which is stored with your Google account. This is governed by Google's privacy policy.
- **Google Play Billing.** Purchases are made and recorded by Google Play. The game checks the purchase receipt Google Play signs, on your device, before delivering what you bought. We do not receive your payment details.
- **Google AdMob (rewarded ads only).** If you choose to watch a rewarded ad, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, an approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interaction data and diagnostic data. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. Ad content is limited to a Teen rating.

The game does not include an analytics or crash-reporting SDK. If the game crashes, Google Play may give us anonymous crash and performance statistics (Android vitals) under Google's terms.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise location or messages.

## How information is used

- To run the game and keep your progress, on your device and (if you sign in) in your Play Games cloud save.
- To deliver purchases and subscriptions, and to restore them on a new device.
- To show a rewarded ad, only when you choose to watch one.
- To send local reminders (away time full, research done, an event starting) only if you turn them on in Settings. They are scheduled on your device; no push service is used.

## Sharing

We do not run servers that receive your data, we do not sell personal information, and we do not share it. Google processes data for the services above under its own terms.

## Deleting your data

- In the game: Settings → Delete my data. This deletes your Play Games cloud save and then the save, backups and settings on your device.
- Uninstalling the game deletes everything stored on your device.
- Without the game installed: you can delete Play Games data for the game from your Google account (Play Games app → Settings → Delete Play Games account and data), and follow the steps on our [data deletion page](delete-account).

Purchases are recorded by Google Play under your Google account; we hold no copy.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us.

## Security

Data the Google services exchange with the game is encrypted in transit. Your save on the device is kept in the game's private storage.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to or restrict its processing. Because we hold no copy of your data, most requests are met by the in-game deletion or by Google's account tools; contact us if you need help.
